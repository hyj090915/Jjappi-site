## Design Context

### Users
Friends, family and fans who use a Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14+) and like the Chiikawa characters. They land here from a shared link, want to know in a few seconds what 짜삐 is, then download and open it.

### Brand Personality
Puffy, mischievous, homemade. Like a balloon or cotton candy: soft, round, light. Warm and a little playful, never corporate.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light cream page (daytime, casual browsing), warm brown text, one cotton-candy pink accent. Round puffy Korean display type. The characters themselves are the decoration: flat 2D art with brown outlines, standing on edges and peeking in, like they do in the app. Anti-references: AI landing pages (gradients, feature card grids, centered hero with two buttons, gray caption text everywhere, emoji headings).

### Design Principles
1. Characters do the talking: real character art in many poses instead of icons or decoration.
2. Every word earns its place: no small gray captions, no restated headings.
3. Soft and round in type and character, but layouts stay calm, left-aligned and asymmetric.
4. One accent color, used rarely.
5. Motion only where a character reacts (hover hop, expression swap), and none with reduced motion.
